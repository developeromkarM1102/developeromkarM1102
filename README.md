## About

I'm a third-year Information Technology student at the University of Mumbai and a full-stack developer focused on building AI-powered web applications.

I work across the **MERN** stack and **Next.js**. On the backend, I build **REST APIs** with **Node.js** and **Express**, manage data in **MongoDB and MySQL**, and test my endpoints in **Postman**. I integrate the **AI APIs** into real applications, verify those integrations in Postman too, and care about making AI output dependable through structured responses and validation. I solve problems in **Java** to strengthen my **DSA** fundamentals and I’m building cloud experience with **AWS and Docker**. I’m always working to build scalable, real-world projects.

Open to internships and fresher roles in full-stack and software engineering.
